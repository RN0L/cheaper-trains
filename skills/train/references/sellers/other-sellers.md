# Other sellers and operators

Short status for every other seller or operator the research touched. Confidence tags as in [../method.md](../method.md): [verified YYYY-MM-DD], [seen YYYY-MM-DD], [reported], [unverified], [hypothesis]. Most are skips: do not spend searches on them. The few that are only unverified say when a single test is worth it.

## RegioJet

Status: skip [reported].

- No rail service in Germany in 2026. Only RegioJet buses run Berlin - Dresden - Prag.
- The Kyiv - Berlin/Hannover rail plans announced in 2023 are not reported as running.
- When to try: never for a train trip. A RegioJet bus is a different product; mention it only if the user asks about buses.

## Leo Express

Status: skip [reported].

- Holds an EBA certificate (German rail safety approval) from 11/2020 but runs no service in Germany.
- When to try: never, unless a German service is announced.

## Italo

Status: skip [reported].

- No service in Germany. For Italy-bound trains from München see [trenitalia.md](trenitalia.md), which is also a documented dead end for German legs.
- When to try: never.

## Snälltåget

Status: unverified; low priority [reported].

- Night train Stockholm / Malmö - København - Padborg - Hamburg - Berlin. On some 2025 dates it was extended to Dresden and Chemnitz.
- Day train IC 306/307 Stockholm - Malmö - København - Hamburg since 04.05.2026, with a stop seen at Rendsburg.
- Its policy on domestic German legs is unverified. SBB cannot sell its tickets.
- When to try: only as a separate-train anchor when the user's pair is Hamburg - Berlin (night) or lies on Hamburg - Rendsburg, and its departure fits W. One search on its own site; record 'domestic not sold' if refused.

## SJ EuroNight (Stockholm - Hamburg - Berlin)

Status: not checked [unverified].

- A night train Stockholm - Hamburg - Berlin. Its German stops and whether it sells German pairs were not checked.
- When to try: as for Snälltåget, only for a night window on Hamburg - Berlin, and only after the main sellers.

## CFL (IC 37 Düsseldorf - Luxembourg)

Status: skip for price [reported].

- IC 37: Düsseldorf - Köln - Bonn - Remagen - Andernach - Koblenz - Cochem - Bullay - Wittlich - Trier - Wasserbillig - Luxembourg, one pair a day.
- It runs as RE on the Mosel line with CFL KISS units, and its German legs are mostly regional-priced, so regional fares or the Deutschlandticket already apply.
- Koblenz - Düsseldorf is suspended 10.07-12.12.2026.
- When to try: not for German pairs. DB Sparpreis Europa to Luxembourg on bahn.de covers the rest.

## SŽ, HŽPP and ŽPCG (Slovenia, Croatia, Montenegro)

Status: skip [seen 2026-09-26].

- In the Berlin-München session none of them offered online international sales from Germany.
- When to try: never for German legs. For the München - Ljubljana - Zagreb EC use [oebb.md](oebb.md) or bahn.de.

## FlixTrain

Status: separate-train anchor only [reported].

- Own tickets with dynamic pricing, sold on flixtrain.de. DB tickets are not valid on it, and SBB cannot sell it.
- Routes per Wikipedia, which may be stale: Berlin - Köln, Hamburg - Köln, Berlin - Stuttgart, Hamburg - Berlin - Leipzig, München - Frankfurt, Berlin - Basel.
- When to try: when the user's pair is on one of these routes, as one search for a price anchor. Compare it on its own departure, arrival and duration. Never present it as the same-train trick.

## Routes that do not exist

So the skill never searches for them:

- Hamburg - Puttgarden - Rødby by train: ended 14.12.2019. All Hamburg - København trains run via Flensburg ([dsb.md](dsb.md)).
- A long-distance Berlin - Szczecin train: none. The EC ended in 2012 and the leg is regional ([pkp-intercity.md](pkp-intercity.md)).
- A Nürnberg - Praha long-distance through train: none found for 2026 ([cd.md](cd.md)).
- Hamburg - Kiel on a foreign-priced train: none; ICE 22 is domestic.
- ÖBB Nightjets to Paris ended 14.12.2025, and Berlin - Brussels ended in March 2025. European Sleeper runs Paris - Berlin now ([european-sleeper.md](european-sleeper.md)).
