# ÖBB ticket shop (and Nightjet)

Terms (O, D, W, T, K, key train, ticket ends, the record schema) are defined in [../method.md](../method.md). Confidence tags: [verified YYYY-MM-DD] = seen on the seller's own offer page; [seen YYYY-MM-DD] = list view or a single observation; [reported] = third-party source; [unverified]; [hypothesis]. Every price here is an observation with a date, never a promise.

## Status

- **The strongest proven lever** [verified 2026-09-26/27]. Use it for any key train that feeds München, Rosenheim, Passau or Lindau toward Austria. In the reference session every cheap result came from this shop.
- The key train does **not** have to cross the border itself. ÖBB prices the whole through-journey, including purely domestic DB feeder trains: ICE 1507 Berlin → München was sold inside a Berlin Hbf → Kitzbühel ticket.
- It is **not** a domestic German seller in its own right. A standalone domestic German Sparschiene (for example München → Nürnberg) is unconfirmed.

## What it sells

- **Through-tickets from German stations to Austrian destinations** (and back) that **include** the DB ICE/IC legs, priced from ÖBB's own Sparschiene contingents. These are not DB's contingents: ÖBB still sold 2nd class on ICEs that bahn.de showed as 'Zug ausgebucht' (fully booked).
- Tariff basis: ÖBB Handbuch für Reisen mit den ÖBB ins Ausland, Version 2026.03, valid from 07.09.2026, B.1.3.1 "Sparschiene ... nach Deutschland mit der Deutschen Bahn". It is sold for lines of DB and of non-federal railways in tariff cooperation with DB (B.1.3.1.4.2), up to 6 months ahead and until 1 minute before departure (B.1.3.1.2).
- **Sparschiene Europa** is advertised from 2nd class 'ab 19 €' ("from 19 €") [reported, Trainline] and goes on sale up to about 6 months ahead. The route Innsbruck-Garmisch-München is excluded from it [reported, oebb.at].
- **Nightjets** are sold only at ÖBB tariff (since 10.06.2018, DB-tariff through-tickets are no longer recognised on them) [reported, de.wikipedia]. So domestic Nightjet legs such as Hamburg → München are ÖBB-priced even when bought on bahn.de, which advertises Hamburg-München from 34,90 € [reported]. The tariff lists nightjet.com as a sales point (B.1.3.1.10).
- **Resellers.** Trainline resells ÖBB fares at the same price (Villach 62,60 € on both sites) [seen 2026-09-26]. See [trainline.md](trainline.md).

## Fares and conditions

The shop labels its offers NON-FLEX, SEMI-FLEX and FLEX. Those labels do not appear in the tariff. Mapping them to tariff products is an inference from the refund rules.

| Shop label | Tariff product (inferred) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| NON-FLEX | Sparschiene | Train-bound: the offer says 'Verkehrsmittel sind fix: ...' ("the trains are fixed: ..."). Not refundable before or after the first validity day (B.1.3.1.9). Seat reservation optional: +3,00 € with one long-distance train on the ticket (ICE 1507 [verified 2026-09-26], ICE 1185 [seen 2026-09-28]), +6,00 € with two (ICE 583 + RJX 67) [seen 2026-09-28]; likely 3,00 € per reservable train [hypothesis]. |
| SEMI-FLEX | Sparschiene Komfort | Reservation included "wenn sie zum Buchungszeitpunkt technisch verfügbar ist" (if technically available at booking, B.1.2.1.3.1). The offer text said 50 % back until the day before. The tariff adds a free refund until 15 days before the first validity day, 50 % fee until 1 day before, nothing from the first validity day (B.1.2.1.9). |
| FLEX | Standard | Free choice of train and free cancellation according to the offer text. 225,00 € to Kitzbühel [verified 2026-09-26]; about 218-236 € across the destinations tried [seen 2026-09-26]. |

Rules that apply to Sparschiene to Germany (B.1.3.1.3-B.1.3.1.5), and that the user must hear before buying (full list in [../tariff-rules.md](../tariff-rules.md)):

- Valid only on the validity day and in the booked long-distance and cross-border regional trains. Inside Austria, regional trains before and after the long-distance trains may be earlier or later.
- "Sie können Ihre Fahrt zwischendurch nicht unterbrechen" (you cannot break your journey on the way).
- Valid only along the booked route, which is printed on the ticket.
- Personal ticket: the named travellers show photo ID with proof of age. A mismatch counts as travelling without a valid ticket.
- A missed connection caused by a delay: continue with the next possible trains to the ticket's destination (B.1.3.1.3.5).
- **Getting off early and boarding late are not written rights.** The tariff has no clause forbidding them, and the offer text did not mention them either way. Disclose this risk every time.

**Customer groups.** The tariff lists "Kinder, Fahrzeuge und Erwachsene" (children, vehicles and adults) for Sparschiene to Germany (B.1.3.1.11.1), and the domestic handbook defines "Erwachsene sind Reisende ab dem 15. Geburtstag" (adults are travellers from their 15th birthday, A.1.3.1.1). The shop still offers a 'Jugendliche:r' (youth) passenger type. Whether a youth price differs from the adult price is unverified.

**Price tiers seen** (contingent levels, not time-of-day effects): 54,00 / 62,60 / 77,20 / 86,90 / 96,20 / 105,70 / 115,40 / 130,00 / 144,30 € [seen 2026-09-26/27]. The price button carries the label '1. Klasse' when 2nd class is gone. The cheapest option on a train may then be a 1st-class Sparschiene (often 115,40 €). Prices move fast: 1st class on the ICE 1507 offer rose from 94,60 € to 130,00 € within one hour on Sun 27.09.2026 [verified 2026-09-27].

## Where the lever is

**Principle.** The ticket must end beyond the DB tariff points and beyond the point where DB hands over to ÖBB. DB counts Salzburg Hbf, Kufstein and the Außerfernbahn as DB-domestic (SCIC-NRT), so tickets to them come back at DB prices. Prices do not rise steadily with distance, so scan families of ticket ends, not single stations. Corridor lists: [../corridors.md](../corridors.md).

- **Through ICEs to Salzburg, Klagenfurt, Graz, Innsbruck and Kitzbühel** (lines 62 and 24) and **Nightjets**: see [../corridors.md](../corridors.md).

**Published evidence** [reported]:

- appgefahren, 23.09.2025: Essen → Salzburg Mülln via ÖBB Sparschiene 62,60 € against DB Essen → München 139,99 €, on an "absolut identische" (absolutely identical) connection. It also mentions Ulm, Stuttgart and Memmingen as origins.
- mydealz, 16.06.2025: Köln → Innsbruck bought at ÖBB, getting off in München, "über 130 €" (over 130 €) saved; "funktioniert nicht auf allen Strecken" (does not work on every route).

**Where it did not work.** From Berlin, ÖBB tickets to Italy, Switzerland, Slovenia, Croatia and Hungary were mostly partial tickets ('Teilstrecke') or expensive (Budapest 135,00 € in 1st class, 327,60 € in 2nd class) [seen 2026-09-26]. For the Berlin → München trip, making the German end longer (a ticket starting in Hamburg, Rostock or Gesundbrunnen, or ending in Hamburg-Altona or Spandau) never beat the Berlin-origin fare. This says nothing about trips that really start elsewhere: for Hamburg → München, price Hamburg Hbf → Puch b.Hallein / Kitzbühel Bahnhof, Via München Hbf, as in the Berlin case.

## How to search

The verified flow [verified 2026-09-26/27]. Everything happens in the skill's own tab(s); never touch the user's tabs.

1. Open https://shop.oebbtickets.at/de/ticket. A Cloudflare 'Sicherheitsüberprüfung' (security check) page passes by itself in about 10 s. Never solve or click through a challenge; if it does not pass within about 20 s, stop and hand over to the user.
2. Close the 'Anmelden / Neu? Starten Sie hier' (log in / new? start here) popup. It returns after **every** page load. If a coordinate click on its X is ignored, close it via its aria-label close button.
3. **Von / Nach** (from / to): type the name, wait about 2 s, and click the exact suggestion. For example, typing 'Puch' offers 'Puch b.Hallein Urstein Bahnhst' and 'Puch b.Hallein': pick the entry that reads exactly 'Puch b.Hallein' (first on 2026-09-26, second on 2026-09-28). Schwarzach-St. Veit is 'Schwarzach im Pongau-St.Veit Bahnhof' in the shop. Shop spellings are listed in [../stations.md](../stations.md) (for example 'Berlin Hbf (Tiefgeschoß)', 'Kitzbühel Bahnhof', 'Bernau a Chiemsee').
4. **Via:** '+ Via hinzufügen' (add via), type D (outbound) or the boarding station (return), pick the suggestion. Remove it with 'Via entfernen'. For a via-feeder variant, enter the feeder station instead (for example 'Bernau a Chiemsee').
5. **Date:** use the arrows beside the date field, then read the field to verify the day.
6. **Time:** triple-click the time field and type HHMM: the start of W, not 0400. One search plus at most 3 'Spätere Verbindungen' clicks covers about 3-4 h of departures; run one more search per further 3-4 h of W.
7. **Set the travellers, before EVERY search** (the box resets between sessions and sometimes between searches). The passenger box appears on the start page only after Von and Nach are filled, and the traveller dialog is its own page (/de/ticket/person/...) [seen 2026-09-28]:
   1. Click the passenger box (default '1 x Erwachsene:r', one adult).
   2. Every traveller aged 6-26: click the X on the 'Erwachsene:r' card, click '+ KIND / JUGEND' (child / youth), and type the exact age into the 'Alter*' field, which is free text. The age counts 'am Reisetag' (on the travel day), and the shop takes that day from the date then in the form, so set the date (step 5) first [seen 2026-09-28]. Travellers aged 27 or over: keep '1 x Erwachsene:r'.
   3. Click the small red 'OK' on the card, then the big 'OK' of the dialog.
   4. Before every search, read the box text and copy it verbatim into the row's `warnings` and into How-to-book step 2.
   5. Only age 19 → '1 x Jugendliche:r ohne Ermäßigungen' (one youth, no discounts) is verified. For ages 20-26, price the finalist once more as 'Erwachsene:r' and keep the cheaper one; mark the difference [unverified].
8. Set the 'ab/an' toggle (departure/arrival), click 'Suchen' (search), and wait about 15 s.
9. **Read the list** by pasting [../../scripts/oebb-results.js](../../scripts/oebb-results.js) into `mcp__claude-in-chrome__javascript_tool`. Set its `N` (the number of 'Spätere Verbindungen' clicks, 9 s each) to 3 or less; the tool call times out after about 45 s. The tool also cuts a result after about 1,200 characters ('[TRUNCATED]'): if that happens, rerun with N=0 and a FROM/TO departure filter. For departures beyond the 3-4 h this covers, run a new search with a later time.
10. **Offer page:** click the price button 'ab € X' of a promising row. It opens `/de/ticket/offer`. Read it with [../../scripts/oebb-offer.js](../../scripts/oebb-offer.js) or `get_page_text`, then go back to the list. If the list comes back as 'Keine Reise gefunden' (no journey found), wait 40-60 s, re-confirm the passenger, and search again.
11. **Record** one row per priced option (schema in [../method.md](../method.md)): `seller` = 'ÖBB', `ticket_from`, `ticket_to`, `via`, `all_trains` (from 'Verkehrsmittel sind fix'), `changes`, `direct_on_user_leg`, `partial_ticket`, `class`, `fare` (for example 'Sparschiene NON-FLEX'), `price_eur`, `price_raw`, `offer_page_checked`.

## Reading results and the offer page

**Results list** (what [../../scripts/oebb-results.js](../../scripts/oebb-results.js) returns: departure, trains, changes, price label, warnings, partial flag):

- **'N Umstiege: <stations>'** (N changes: stations) drives the directness test. The key train must be the only long-distance train between O and D:
  - 'ICE1003, RJX65, IC795 | 2 Umstiege: München Hbf, Salzburg Hbf' is direct on Berlin → München (the first change is at D).
  - 'ICE1005, ICE505, RJ87 | 2 Umstiege: Nürnberg Hbf, München Hbf' is **not** direct (a change in Nürnberg).
  - When the change stations are not printed, open 'Reisedetails' (journey details) for per-station times, including the arrival and departure at D.
- **Price labels:** 'Sparschiene' = a saver fare; '1. Klasse' = the 'ab' price is 1st class; 'Ticket nicht verfügbar' = none; 'Standard' = only the FLEX fare is left.
- **'ACHTUNG: Starker Reisetag/Mitfahrt nur mit Sitzplatzreservierung gesichert'** (warning: heavy travel day, travel only guaranteed with a seat reservation). Flag the row: the user may have to stand, or may not be let on.
- **One departure, several rows.** The same departure can appear in several rows with different routings and prices: on Hamburg -> Kitzbühel, ICE 1185 at 09:21 showed '0 Umstiege' Sparschiene 62,60 € and, as a separate row, an RJ 89 routing via München Gl.5-10 at 227,60 € with a peak warning [seen 2026-09-28]. Record each row. When a key train itself runs past D (e.g. the line-24 ICE 1185 to Kitzbühel and Schwarzach), price ends on its own run first; the 0-change row can be the cheapest, and the same train can come back as a partial ticket on another end (it did to Puch b.Hallein).
- **'Ticket nur für Teilstrecke: X -> Y'** (ticket only for part of the route): a partial ticket. Discard it. In the page text this note comes **before** its own connection, so a naive split assigns it to the previous row. The extractor reads it from the right place; do not "fix" that.

**Offer page** (`/de/ticket/offer`):

- '2. Klasse ab' and '1. Klasse ab' (2nd / 1st class from).
- 'Nur Sitzplatz reservieren (kein Ticket)' (reserve a seat only, no ticket).
- The offers NON-FLEX, SEMI-FLEX and FLEX with their texts.
- 'Verkehrsmittel sind fix: ...' followed by the fixed trains.
- The route line, for example 'Berlin Hbf (Tiefgeschoß) › Kitzbühel Bahnhof'. It must show the full route and no 'Teilstrecke'.

**STOP LINE.** The purchase flow continues 'Angebot wählen' (choose offer) → 'Weiter zur Reservierung' (continue to reservation) → passenger names → cart (`POST /api/order/v5/shoppingCart`). The reservation is attempted only at the cart step, and it can fail there:

- 11149 'gewünschter Sitzplatz ... nicht verfügbar' (requested seat not available)
- 11154 '... Platzlage ...' (seat position)

**Automation never goes past the offer page.** It never clicks 'Angebot wählen', never enters a name and never logs in. The cart and login live in localStorage and are shared by every tab of the browser, including the one the user may be booking in. Report "seat not guaranteed" instead of testing the reservation, and check seats on bahn.de with 'Nur Sitzplatz buchen' (see [db-bahn-de.md](db-bahn-de.md)).

**For the user's own booking**, the reservation page offered 'Wagenauswahl: Großraumwagen 2. Klasse' or 'Großraumwagen Ruhezone 2. Klasse' (car: open-plan 2nd class, or open-plan quiet zone) and 'Bevorzugter Sitzplatz: ohne bestimmten Sitzplatzwunsch' or 'mit Tisch' (seat: no preference, or with a table) [seen 2026-09-26].

## Limits and bot protection

The binding rules are in [../automation-rules.md](../automation-rules.md). For the ÖBB shop specifically:

- **Terms.** ÖBB's Nutzungsbedingungen have no explicit anti-bot clause, but use must match the purpose the content is provided for, and content may not be exploited. robots.txt on shop.oebbtickets.at allows only `/`, `/de|en|it/ticket*` and `/shop*`. Stay on those pages and never call `/api/...` directly.
- **At most 2-3 ÖBB tabs in total**, across the main session and every agent. With about 9 parallel tabs the shop answered HTTP 429 (too many requests) on `/api/offer/v2/travelActions`, and it slowed the user's own booking [seen 2026-09-26]. The symptoms of a 429:
  - 'Suchen' silently does nothing;
  - price buttons stay empty;
  - 'Keine Reise gefunden';
  - 'Reisedetails' dialogs load slowly or show an earlier request.

  **Back-off:** wait 40-60 s, re-confirm the passenger ('ändern' then OK), and search again. A `hafas/v4/timetable` 502 shows up the same way, or as 'Mit Ihren Angaben konnten keine Tickets gefunden werden' (no tickets found for your details).
- **Storage.** The search state lives in sessionStorage per tab. Login and cart live in localStorage, shared by all tabs.
- **Background tabs are throttled.** 'Reisedetails' dialogs may render empty until a screenshot forces a render.
- **The JavaScript tool times out after about 45 s.** Keep awaited loops short (N ≤ 3).
- **Clicks.** The first coordinate click after a full page load was often ignored. Click again or use the element's accessible name.

## Unverified: test before relying on it

- Whether the youth price ('Jugendliche:r') differs from the adult price ('Erwachsene:r').
- The ÖBB youth age range in the shop: which ages '+ KIND / JUGEND' accepts, and what the box then reads (only age 19 was recorded).
- The Passau corridor (ICE 91, ticket ends Schärding, Linz or further).
- The Lindau-Bregenz corridor from Stuttgart, Ulm or Memmingen (via München it was partly seen, see above).
- Domestic German sales: a Sparschiene between two German stations.
- Any written ÖBB statement that getting off early, or boarding after the ticket's origin, is tolerated. The offer text did not say so, and the tariff neither allows nor forbids it (see [../tariff-rules.md](../tariff-rules.md)).
- Whether an ÖBB reservation succeeds on a given train. That is known only at the cart step, which automation never reaches.
- Whether the reservation surcharge is exactly 3,00 € per reservable train (+3,00 € with one long-distance train, +6,00 € with two, seen 2026-09-28) [hypothesis].
- The flow for discount cards (ÖBB Vorteilscard, BahnCard) in the passenger dialog.
