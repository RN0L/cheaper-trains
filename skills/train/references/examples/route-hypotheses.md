# Route hypotheses beyond Berlin - München

Worked "how to think about this route" examples, including a domestic trunk into a hub (7) and routes with no lever (6). Each one runs Phases 2-4 of [../method.md](../method.md) on paper and follows the same structure:

- **Key trains (line):** the trains that run O -> D without a change.
- **Where they continue:** beyond D, or from before O.
- **Tariff point:** the DB tariff point and the carrier-change point from [../corridors.md](../corridors.md).
- **Sellers to ask.**
- **Ticket to try:** from -> to, via, where to get on or off.
- **DB levers:** what bahn.de itself can do.
- **Expected verdict:** a prior, not a promise.
- **What would falsify it.**
- **Playbooks to open.**

Only the Berlin - München session is verified ([berlin-muenchen-2026-09.md](berlin-muenchen-2026-09.md)). Everything here is [hypothesis] unless a tag says otherwise. Line routes are from the 2026 de.wikipedia line lists [reported]. Getting off early and boarding late carry the risks listed in [../tariff-rules.md](../tariff-rules.md).

## 1a. Stuttgart -> München

**Key trains (line)**
- Line 62, ICE 4 (Frankfurt - Heidelberg - Stuttgart - Ulm - Günzburg - Augsburg - München).
- Line 11 (Berlin - ... - Mannheim - Stuttgart - Ulm - Augsburg - München-Pasing - München).
- Line 60 (Basel SBB - Freiburg - Offenburg - Karlsruhe - Stuttgart - Ulm - Augsburg - München; two pairs).
- Line 83, one TGV/ICE pair Paris - Strasbourg - Karlsruhe - Stuttgart - Ulm - Augsburg - München.
- ICE 224/225 (Amsterdam - Köln Messe/Deutz - Frankfurt Flughafen - Mannheim - Stuttgart - Ulm - Augsburg - München).
- WESTbahn Stuttgart - Ulm - Günzburg - Augsburg - München Hbf (two pairs a day): a separate train, not the same-train lever.

**Where they continue**
- Three line-62 ICE 4 pairs run on through München - Rosenheim - Salzburg to Villach, Klagenfurt and Graz.
- From München every key train can feed the Salzburg line (RE5, RJX, Railjet) and the Kufstein line (RB54, line 89).
- For boarding later: line 83 comes from Strasbourg, and line 60 comes from Basel SBB.

**Tariff point**
- Salzburg Hbf and Kufstein are DB-domestic, so the ticket must end beyond them.
- Kehl(Gr) for line 83.
- Basel Bad Bf is DB-domestic for line 60, so start at Basel SBB.

**Sellers to ask:** ÖBB first; DB Sparpreis Europa (AT, FR, CH); SBB; SNCF Connect [unverified]; WESTbahn.

**Tickets to try**
1. ÖBB: Stuttgart Hbf -> Puch b.Hallein, Via München Hbf; get off in München. Also Stuttgart Hbf -> Kitzbühel Bahnhof, Via München Hbf. appgefahren names Ulm, Stuttgart and Memmingen as origins for the ÖBB trick [reported].
2. The same with the via-feeders from the session: Via 'Bernau a Chiemsee' (Salzburg line) and Via 'Oberaudorf' (Kufstein line).
3. Line 83: ticket Strasbourg -> München on the one through TGV/ICE, boarding in Stuttgart. DB Sparpreis Europa France from 19,99 €, sold until the day before; SNCF [unverified].
4. Line 60: ticket Basel SBB -> München, boarding in Stuttgart (SBB, or DB Sparpreis Europa Switzerland from 19,99 €).
5. WESTbahn as a separate-train anchor: the DeutschlandPlusTicket needs a valid personal Deutschland-Ticket [reported, westbahn.at]. Compare it on its own speed.

**DB levers**
- Non-Sprinter trains (`s=false`).
- Young or Senior fares by exact age.
- DB Sparpreis Europa Austria on a line-62 through ICE 4, to the first stop after Salzburg (Golling-Abtenau); get off in München.
- One station beyond München on DB (München Ost).

**Expected verdict:** likely to work on days when the domestic contingent on the key train is sold out, as in the Berlin case. The leg is shorter and the domestic floor is 17,99 € (Super Sparpreis), so absolute savings are smaller on normal days.

**What would falsify it**
- ÖBB returns only 'Teilstrecke', 'DB-Tarif' or 'Ticket nicht verfügbar' for Stuttgart origins.
- ÖBB routes the ticket via other trains than the key train (check the fixed trains).
- The ÖBB price sits above DB's Super Sparpreis on the same train across several ticket ends.

**Playbooks:** [../sellers/oebb.md](../sellers/oebb.md), [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md), [../sellers/westbahn.md](../sellers/westbahn.md), [../sellers/sncf-connect.md](../sellers/sncf-connect.md), [../sellers/sbb.md](../sellers/sbb.md).

## 1b. Karlsruhe -> München

**Key trains (line):** line 60 (2 pairs) and the line-83 TGV (1 pair) only. Lines 62, 11 and ICE 224/225 do not call at Karlsruhe.

**Where they continue:** as in 1a, from München onto the Salzburg and Kufstein lines. Before O, line 60 comes from Basel SBB and the TGV from Strasbourg.

**Tickets to try**
1. ÖBB: Karlsruhe Hbf -> Puch b.Hallein, Via München Hbf. Keep only rows whose first train is the line-60 ICE or the TGV; everything else changes in Stuttgart or Mannheim and fails direct-only.
2. Boarding-late shapes: Basel SBB -> München (line 60; SBB or DB Europa CH) and Strasbourg -> München (TGV; DB Europa FR, not sold on the travel day).

**Expected verdict:** as 1a, but with 3 key trains a day the window often holds none: then SKILL.md Phase 2 step 4 applies.

**Playbooks:** as 1a.

## 2. Frankfurt, Köln or Hamburg -> Nürnberg or Regensburg (ICE 91 to Wien)

**Key trains (line)**
- Line 91, ICE T: Dortmund - Köln - Bonn - Koblenz - Mainz - Frankfurt Flughafen - Frankfurt - Hanau - Aschaffenburg - Würzburg - Nürnberg - Regensburg - Plattling - Passau - Schärding - Wels - Linz - Wien. One pair runs from Hamburg via Hannover - Göttingen - Kassel - Fulda - Würzburg.
- ICE 92/93 and ICE 94/95: Hamburg - Berlin - Halle - Erfurt - Coburg - Nürnberg - Regensburg - Straubing/Plattling - Passau - Wien.

**Where they continue:** Passau -> Schärding -> Wels -> Linz -> St. Pölten -> Wien, the same train.

**Tariff point:** the carrier change is at Passau Hbf (SCIC-NRT B.13.1). The ticket must end in Austria; Schärding is the first stop past the border.

**Sellers to ask:** ÖBB; DB Sparpreis Europa Austria (from 19,99 €); Trainline as a cross-check.

**Tickets to try**
1. ÖBB: Frankfurt(Main)Hbf -> Schärding, then -> Linz/Donau Hbf, Via Nürnberg Hbf; get off in Nürnberg. For Regensburg: Via Regensburg Hbf; get off there.
2. Return: Linz/Donau Hbf -> Frankfurt(Main)Hbf, Via Nürnberg Hbf; board in Nürnberg.
3. From Köln or Hamburg: the same ends with the matching origin.
4. ICE 92-95: Hamburg Hbf -> Linz, Via Nürnberg Hbf; get off in Nürnberg.

**DB levers:** DB Sparpreis Europa Austria to Schärding on the same ICE; non-Sprinter alternatives; Young/Senior.

**Expected verdict:** plausible on sold-out days. **Caveat:** Regensburg - Wien has only limited service from 14.06. to 13.12.2026 (corridor renovation). Steps:
1. In Phase 1, open the key train's connection details on bahn.de for the travel date and check whether it runs through Passau to Linz, ends early, or shows a bus replacement.
2. If it runs through, sweep ÖBB O -> Schärding / Linz via D as planned.
3. If it does not, run one ÖBB search O -> Linz/Donau Hbf via D. If ÖBB routes via München or a bus, the row fails the directness test: record 'Passau corridor closed by works 14.06-13.12.2026' and go to the München corridors (for Regensburg or Passau as D, a München-routed ticket works only if the user accepts the change) or to the domestic-only playbook ([../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md)).

**What would falsify it**
- ÖBB sells only a 'Teilstrecke' from Passau.
- ÖBB routes the ticket via München instead of the ICE 91 (not the key train).
- The Sparschiene is never below DB's domestic Super Sparpreis on the same train.

**Playbooks:** [../sellers/oebb.md](../sellers/oebb.md), [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md), [../sellers/trainline.md](../sellers/trainline.md).

## 3. Berlin -> Dresden (RJ line 27)

**Key trains (line)**
- Line 27, ČD ComfortJet/railjet: Berlin Hbf (tief) - Berlin Südkreuz - Elsterwerda - Dresden-Neustadt - Dresden Hbf - Bad Schandau - Děčín - Praha (- Brno - Wien).
- RJ 384/385 København - Hamburg - Berlin - Dresden - Praha.
- IC 2173/2175 Rostock Hbf – Flughafen BER - Terminal 1-2 – Dresden Hbf, every 2 h; from Berlin Hbf only with an R to Flughafen BER (08:21 -> 10:28, 10:19 -> 12:28) [seen 2026-09-28]; a key connection only when changes are allowed.
- ICE 1970 Dresden - Berlin Gesundbrunnen (since 11.07.2026) is a domestic DB train. It can still be a feeder: try DB Sparpreis Europa or ČD Děčín hl.n. -> Berlin via Dresden, if the seller routes onto ICE 1970 [hypothesis].

**Where they continue:** Dresden -> Bad Schandau -> Děčín hl.n. -> Ústí nad Labem -> Praha, normally the same train; see the caveat in [../corridors.md](../corridors.md) 'Timetable caveats 2026' (on Fr 02.10.2026 the Berlin RJs ended in Dresden).

**Tariff point:** Schöna(Gr), between Bad Schandau and Děčín (SCIC-NRT B.20.1). The ticket must end at Děčín hl.n. or beyond.

**Sellers to ask**
- ČD. It needs a cross-border train, so the ticket is Berlin -> a Czech end (Ústí nad Labem hl.n., Děčín hl.n. or Praha hl.n.), getting off in Dresden. ČD's cheap fare may not apply from German origins [reported, cd.cz]; it was seen once on routings through ČD's own cross-border train (Evidence).
- DB Sparpreis Europa Czech Republic: from 14,99 €; Young from 13,99 €; sold until the day before. This is the likelier lever.
- European Sleeper Berlin - Dresden: a night train, 3 times a week; whether it sells the domestic leg is unclear [unconfirmed].

**Tickets to try**
1. DB Sparpreis Europa: Berlin Hbf -> Děčín hl.n. on the key RJ; get off in Dresden Hbf.
2. ČD: 'Berlin Hbf' (the results show 'Berlin Hbf (tief)') -> Ústí nad Labem hl.n., Děčín hl.n. and Praha hl.n.; get off in Dresden. The end decides whether ČD routes through its own cross-border train.
3. Return: Děčín hl.n. -> Berlin Hbf; board in Dresden.

**DB levers:** the domestic floors are low (Super Sparpreis from 17,99 €, Super Sparpreis Young from 4,99 €), so compare against the domestic fare on the same RJ first.

**Evidence**
- travel-dealz, 31.03.2025: Berlin - Děčín about 17 € with ČD against 13,99 € with DB [reported].
- [seen 2026-09-28] Travel Fr 02.10.2026, age 25: DB on RJ 173 / RJ 175 30,99-32,99 € (Trainline proxy); DB Sparpreis Europa to Ústí 36,99-40,99 €, Děčín 43,99-47,99 €, Praha from 42,94 €; ČD Berlin Hbf -> Ústí 689 Kč (about 27,56 € at 25 Kč/EUR) on the same RJs, -> Děčín 1948 Kč, -> Praha 861 Kč. A saving of 3-5 € on the RJs, and only at ČD.

**Expected verdict:** small savings at best. The Europa floor (14,99 €) is barely under the domestic Super Sparpreis floor (17,99 €), and above Super Sparpreis Young.

**What would falsify it**
- DB Sparpreis Europa to Děčín is repeatedly well below the domestic Berlin - Dresden fare on the same RJ on busy days.
- ČD offers a cheap fare from a German origin on a direct RJ.

**Playbooks:** [../sellers/cd.md](../sellers/cd.md), [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md), [../sellers/european-sleeper.md](../sellers/european-sleeper.md).

## 4. Köln <-> Frankfurt (ICE lines 78 and 79)

**Key trains (line)**
- Line 78: Amsterdam - Utrecht - Arnhem - Oberhausen - Duisburg - Düsseldorf - Köln - Siegburg/Bonn - Frankfurt Flughafen - Frankfurt.
- Line 79: Bruxelles - Liège-Guillemins - Aachen - Köln - Frankfurt Flughafen - Frankfurt.
- Domestic ICEs can still be feeders: try SBB Köln Hbf -> Basel SBB via Frankfurt(Main)Hbf and DB Sparpreis Europa Köln Hbf -> Strasbourg via Frankfurt(Main)Hbf, getting off in Frankfurt [hypothesis]. Also ÖBB Köln Hbf -> Linz/Donau Hbf via Frankfurt(Main)Hbf (feeder to ICE 91) [outside the Phase 3 3-h rule; deep mode only, and only after checking that ICE 91 runs through Passau on the date (works 14.06-13.12.2026)].
- Most Köln-Frankfurt fast trains are domestic (ICE 1713, 1513 on 02.10.2026). Lines 78/79 run every 2 h, so NL/BE levers cover at most 3 of about 6 fast trains in a 4-h window.

**Where they continue**
- From Köln, line 78 runs to Arnhem and Amsterdam, crossing at Emmerich.
- From Köln, line 79 runs to Aachen and Liège, Brussels or Antwerpen. New from 07.09.2026: Köln - Antwerpen and Köln - Oostende pairs.

**Tariff point**
- NL: carrier change at Emmerich(Gr) (B.11.1).
- BE: at the Belgian station where the traveller boards or alights (B.1.1).
- The ticket must end in the Netherlands or Belgium.

**Sellers to ask**
- DB Sparpreis Europa Netherlands or Belgium: from 19,99 €.
- NS International: resells the same DB Europa fares.
- SNCB/b-europe [unverified].
- Eurostar Köln - Aachen is not sold domestically [reported] and is not a key train for Köln - Frankfurt anyway.

**Tickets to try**
1. Frankfurt(Main)Hbf -> Arnhem Centraal on line 78; get off in Köln. Zevenaar is not an ICE stop, so a Zevenaar ticket may be routed off the key train: check the fixed trains.
2. Frankfurt(Main)Hbf -> Liège-Guillemins on line 79; get off in Köln.
3. Reverse: Arnhem or Liège -> Frankfurt; board in Köln.

**DB levers:** domestic Super Sparpreis from 17,99 €, below the 19,99 € Europa floor; non-Sprinter trains; Young/Senior.

**Expected verdict:** helps only when the domestic contingents are sold out and a Europa contingent is still open. On normal days the domestic fare wins.

**Evidence** [seen 2026-09-28, travel Fr 02.10.2026, age 25]: Arnhem -> Frankfurt 121,99 € on ICE 121 (boarding in Köln); Liège -> Frankfurt 115,99 / 151,70 € on ICE 11 / 13; SBB Köln -> Basel SBB via Frankfurt CHF 86-128; domestic Super Sparpreis Young 38,99-48,99 € on the same trains (Trainline proxy). The expected verdict held on this normal day.

**What would falsify it**
- Europa fares to Arnhem or Liège undercut the domestic Köln - Frankfurt fare on normal days (verdict too pessimistic).
- On sold-out days the Europa contingent is gone too (verdict too optimistic).

**Playbooks:** [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md), [../sellers/ns-international.md](../sellers/ns-international.md), [../sellers/eurostar-sncb.md](../sellers/eurostar-sncb.md).

## 5. Freiburg -> Frankfurt (ICE 12/20/43, ECE 4/5 and 8/9, ECE 150/151)

**Key trains (line)**
- Line 12: Basel SBB - Basel Bad - Freiburg - Offenburg - Baden-Baden - Karlsruhe - Mannheim - Frankfurt - ... - Berlin.
- Line 20: Basel - Freiburg - Offenburg - Karlsruhe - Mannheim - Frankfurt - Kassel - Hannover - Hamburg. Two pairs run as ECE 4/5 and ECE 8/9 with SBB Giruno sets.
- Line 43: Basel - Freiburg - Baden-Baden - Karlsruhe - Mannheim - **Frankfurt Flughafen** - Köln - ... Hamburg. It does not call at Frankfurt Hbf, so it is a key train only if Frankfurt Flughafen is acceptable.
- ECE 150/151: Milano - Zürich - Basel SBB - Basel Bad - Freiburg - Ringsheim - Karlsruhe - Mannheim - Frankfurt, one pair a day.

**Where they continue:** before O, the key trains come from Basel SBB, and lines 12 and 43 partly from Zürich, Chur, Interlaken or Brig. ECE 150/151 comes from Milano.

**Tariff point:** Basel Bad Bf is DB-domestic and the carrier change DB/SBB, so a ticket from Basel Bad Bf is just a DB ticket. Start at Basel SBB.

**Sellers to ask:** SBB (Sparbillette / Supersparpreis Europa; prices in CHF, convert with rate and date); DB Sparpreis Europa Switzerland from 19,99 €.

**Tickets to try**
1. Basel SBB -> Frankfurt(Main)Hbf on the key train; board in Freiburg(Breisgau) Hbf.
2. Zürich HB -> Frankfurt(Main)Hbf on line 12 or ECE 150/151; board in Freiburg.
3. Reverse for the return: Frankfurt -> Basel SBB; get off in Freiburg.
4. **Book the seat from Freiburg.** A reservation made from Basel lapses if the seat is not taken within 15 minutes after the train leaves Basel, so book the DB reservation-only product from Freiburg (see [../tariff-rules.md](../tariff-rules.md)).

**DB levers:** domestic Super Sparpreis from 17,99 €; Young/Senior; non-Sprinter trains. Split tickets at Karlsruhe or Mannheim rarely win.

**Expected verdict:** possible on sold-out days. The seller prior is low to medium: SBB cost 3-4 times ÖBB in the Berlin - München session.

**What would falsify it**
- The SBB or DB Europa fare from Basel SBB is above the domestic Freiburg - Frankfurt fare on the same train on sold-out days.
- SBB refuses or reroutes the booking.

**Playbooks:** [../sellers/sbb.md](../sellers/sbb.md), [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md), [../sellers/trainline.md](../sellers/trainline.md).

## 6. Negative examples: where there is no lever

Running the method on these should end quickly with DB levers only. That is a correct result, not a failure.

### 6a. Hamburg -> Flensburg

- **Key trains (line):** the ECE Hamburg - Padborg - København (DSB Talgo sets) and RJ 384/385; German intermediate stops [unverified].
- **Where they continue:** Padborg, Kolding, Odense, København.
- **Tariff point:** Flensburg(Gr) (B.2.1).
- **Sellers to ask:** DSB needs a Danish end, so the ticket is Hamburg -> Padborg, getting off in Flensburg. DB Sparpreis Europa Denmark from 28,99 €.
- **DB levers:** domestic Super Sparpreis from 17,99 €; regional trains and the Deutschland-Ticket.
- **Expected verdict:** rarely worth it. The domestic leg is short and the Europa floor (28,99 €) is high.
- **What would falsify it:** Padborg tickets repeatedly below the domestic fare on the same train.
- **Playbooks:** [../sellers/dsb.md](../sellers/dsb.md), [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md).

### 6b. Hamburg -> Kiel

- **Key trains (line):** ICE line 22 (Kiel - Neumünster - Hamburg - ... - Stuttgart) and regional trains.
- **Where they continue:** nowhere abroad. Kiel is a terminus, and line 22 is domestic.
- **Tariff point:** none.
- **Sellers to ask:** none; no foreign-priced train runs Hamburg - Kiel.
- **DB levers only:** Super Sparpreis, Young/Senior, regional trains, Deutschland-Ticket.
- **Expected verdict:** DB levers only.
- **What would falsify it:** a new foreign-priced train on the route.
- **Playbooks:** [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md).

### 6c. Berlin -> Frankfurt (Oder)

- **Key trains (line):** Berlin-Warszawa-Express and the other PKP/DB EC trains to Poland: Berlin Hbf - Berlin Ostbahnhof - Frankfurt (Oder). From June to December 2026 they use Berlin Gesundbrunnen instead of Berlin Hbf [reported].
- **Where they continue:** Rzepin, Poznań, Warszawa, Wrocław, Kraków.
- **Tariff point:** Frankfurt (Oder)(Gr) (B.14.1).
- **Sellers to ask:** PKP Intercity specials start in Poland, so they cannot cover Berlin -> Frankfurt (Oder). DB Sparpreis Europa Poland is from 18,99 €.
- **DB levers:** regional trains or the Deutschland-Ticket usually win on this short leg.
- **Expected verdict:** no lever. PKP only matters for trips to Poznań or beyond, or for the return from Poland.
- **What would falsify it:** a PKP or DB Europa fare to Rzepin or Poznań below the regional fare to Frankfurt (Oder).
- **Playbooks:** [../sellers/pkp-intercity.md](../sellers/pkp-intercity.md), [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md).

## 7. Köln -> Berlin (ICE line 10, domestic trunk into a hub)

**Key trains (line):** line 10 Berlin Ostbahnhof - Berlin - Berlin-Spandau - (Stendal -) Wolfsburg - Hannover - ... - Hamm, then via the Ruhr to Düsseldorf and partly Köln, or via Hagen - Wuppertal to Köln; line 19 Köln - Wuppertal - Hagen - ... - Berlin, every 2 h, without Wolfsburg and Hamm [reported, de.wikipedia].

**Where they continue**
- Beyond D (Berlin) within about 3 h: the EC to Frankfurt (Oder) - Poznań (from Berlin Gesundbrunnen June-December 2026), and the RJ line 27 to Dresden - Děčín.
- Before O (Köln): line 78 from Arnhem, line 79 from Liège.

**Sellers to ask and tickets to try**
1. DB Sparpreis Europa PL: Köln Hbf -> Poznań Gł.; check that the key ICE is in the train list.
2. DB Sparpreis Europa CZ: Köln Hbf -> Děčín hl.n.
3. DB Sparpreis Europa NL: Arnhem Centraal -> Berlin Hbf, boarding in Köln.

Screen all three on Trainline first. Skip PKP (e-IC has no Köln origin online), ČD (its cheap fare does not start in Germany) and NS (it sells the same fares as DB Europa).

**DB levers:** domestic Super Sparpreis from 17,99 € (Young from 4,99 €), `s=false`, Young/Senior, the domestic-only playbook.

**Expected verdict:** DB levers only on normal days; Europa only when the domestic contingents are sold out (economic gate).

**What would falsify it:** Köln -> Poznań Europa below the domestic fare on the same ICE.

**Playbooks:** [../sellers/db-bahn-de.md](../sellers/db-bahn-de.md), [../sellers/trainline.md](../sellers/trainline.md).
